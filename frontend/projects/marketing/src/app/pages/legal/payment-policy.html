Your proposal or agreement with us can set different terms; where it does, it applies instead of this policy.

1. Proposals and prices

We don’t publish fixed prices. Before any work starts, we send a written proposal setting out the services, what’s included and the price. Work starts once you accept it.

  • Ongoing services (for example monthly SEO, social media or reporting) continue month to month until cancelled.
  • One-off projects (for example an SEO audit or an analytics setup) have a fixed scope and price.

Prices are in Canadian dollars and don’t include sales tax. Applicable taxes, such as HST, are added to invoices where the law requires. Your client portal is included at no extra cost.

2. When we invoice

  • Ongoing services are invoiced monthly, at the end of each month, for the work done that month.
  • One-off projects are invoiced when the project is complete. Longer projects may be invoiced in stages, as set out in the proposal.
  • Third-party costs you’ve agreed to, such as paid tools or stock images, are passed on at cost and shown separately on the invoice. We don’t buy anything on your behalf without your approval.

3. Paying invoices

Invoices are due 15 days after the invoice date. The ways to pay are listed on each invoice. We don’t charge interest on late payments.

If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, we may pause work until it’s paid. We’ll tell you before we do. Work resumes once the account is up to date.

If you think an invoice is wrong, tell us as soon as you can. We’ll look into it, and you only need to pay the part that isn’t in question while we sort it out.

4. Changing a service or its price

  • You can ask to change what’s included in an ongoing service at any time. We’ll confirm any change to the price in writing before it applies.
  • If we change the price of an ongoing service ourselves, we’ll give you at least 30 days’ written notice. You can cancel before the new price applies.

5. Cancelling an ongoing service

There’s no long-term contract unless your proposal says so. You can cancel an ongoing service at any time with 30 days’ written notice; email is fine. We’ll confirm the end date.

  • We carry on the agreed work until the end date, and invoice for it as usual. The final invoice covers the work up to the end date.
  • Your accounts (such as Google Analytics, Search Console and social media) stay yours. We hand back or remove our access by the end date.
  • Work you’ve paid for, such as articles, reports and website copy, is yours to keep.

We may also end a service with 30 days’ written notice, or sooner if invoices remain unpaid after we’ve paused work, or if these terms or our terms and conditions are seriously broken.

6. Refunds

Because we invoice after the work is done, you normally pay only for work already delivered. Fees for work already done aren’t refundable. If a proposal includes a payment in advance and we end the service before doing that work, we’ll refund the part that covers work not done.

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under consumer protection law.
