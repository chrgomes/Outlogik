1. What we collect

When you book a consultation or send us a message

The details you enter: your name, email address, and, if you give them, your company, website, phone number, the services you’re interested in, a budget range, the best time to reach you and your message.

When you subscribe to our newsletter

Your email address, a record of your consent (the wording you agreed to and when), when you confirmed your subscription, and when you unsubscribe if you do.

When you use the client portal

If you’re a client, or work for one, and we invite you to the client portal: your name, email address and password (stored only in a scrambled form that can’t be reversed), your sign-in sessions, and the information about your business and websites that we or you keep there. We keep a record of changes made in the portal, with who made them and when.

Information collected automatically

  • Where your visit came from. The first page you visit, the website that linked to us, and any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source). This is kept in your browser only for that visit, and sent to us only if you submit one of our forms.
  • Your IP address and browser type, when you submit a form or sign in to the portal. We use these to prevent spam and abuse, and to keep accounts secure.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your request or message, arrange a consultation, and prepare a proposal.
  • To provide our services and the client portal to our clients.
  • To send you our newsletter, if you’ve asked for it and confirmed.
  • To send you emails about something you did, such as confirming we received your request.
  • To understand which of our marketing works, using where enquiries came from.
  • To keep our website and portal secure, and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it to make automated decisions about you.

3. Your consent

By sending us a form, you agree to us using the details you provide to respond to you. We send our newsletter only to people who ticked the box to ask for it and then confirmed by email. Every newsletter includes a link to unsubscribe. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing or contacting us. If you do, we may no longer be able to provide some services.

7. How we protect it

We use safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only in scrambled form, access limited to the Outlogik staff who need it, records of who changed what in our systems, and protection against spam and repeated sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we’ll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.
