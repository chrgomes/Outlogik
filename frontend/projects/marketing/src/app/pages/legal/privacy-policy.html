3. Google account data

If you connect a Google account to a workspace in the Outlogik app, you choose what Outlogik may read. Outlogik asks for read-only access: it can see your reports, but it can never change anything in your Google account.

What we read from Google

  • Your Google account email address, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • From Google Analytics: the list of properties and accounts you can see, so you can choose one. For the property you choose, daily totals: sessions, visitors, new visitors, engaged sessions, page views and key events; sessions by channel (such as organic search or direct); and sessions referred by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude. These are totals. We don’t receive information about individual visitors to your website.
  • From Google Search Console: the list of sites you’ve verified, so you can choose one. For the site you choose, daily clicks, impressions and average position, and the searches that led to your site most often over the last 28 days. Search terms are what people typed into Google; rarely, they may include a name or other personal details.

How we use it

We use Google data only to show the workspace its own reports in the Outlogik app. We don’t use it for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

Who can see it

  • People in your organization whose role allows it.
  • An agency you’ve shared your workspace with, if you’ve shared it.
  • Outlogik staff, only when you ask us to (for example, because we’re your agency or you’ve asked for support), when we need to investigate a security problem or abuse, or when the law requires it.

How it’s protected and stored

The permission Google gives us to keep reading your data is stored encrypted, and is never shown in the app. The data we copy is stored in Toronto, Canada, with the rest of your account.

How long we keep it

We keep it while Google stays connected. When you disconnect Google in the app, we cancel our access at Google and delete everything we copied from it, straight away. Removing a property or site from what’s synced deletes the data copied from it.

You can also remove Outlogik’s access from your Google account at any time, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data we already copied stays until you disconnect Google in the app, or ask us to delete it.

Google’s policy

Outlogik’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
